Purpose

This SOP defines the standard warm-up and dynamic stretching routine to be completed before the Full-Body Cable Machine Workout (SOP-GYM-CBL-01). A proper warm-up raises core body temperature, increases blood flow to working muscles, lubricates joints, and activates the nervous system — reducing injury risk and improving performance on the main lifts.

2

Warm-Up Structure

The routine has two phases performed in order: Phase A — General Pulse Raiser (light cardio to raise heart rate) followed by Phase B — Dynamic Stretches (active, moving stretches that mobilise the specific joints and muscles used in the cable workout). Do NOT perform long-hold static stretches before lifting — save those for the cool-down.

3

General Guidelines

  • Total warm-up time: approximately 10–12 minutes before every session.
  • Move through a full, comfortable range of motion — never force or bounce into a stretch.
  • Keep dynamic stretches flowing and continuous, not held for long periods.
  • Increase range of motion gradually across the reps of each movement.
  • Stop and inform a trainer if you feel any sharp pain, dizziness, or instability.
  • Static stretching (long holds) belongs in the COOL-DOWN, after training — see the Cool-Down SOP.

Post-Workout Cool-Down (Static Stretching)

After completing the cable workout, perform 5–8 minutes of static stretching — holding each stretch (chest, lats, biceps/triceps, hip flexors, hamstrings, quads, calves) for 20–30 seconds without bouncing. Static stretching is most effective and safest when muscles are already warm, i.e. AFTER training rather than before.

Purpose

This Standard Operating Procedure (SOP) outlines the standardized setup, execution steps, biomechanical guidelines, and prescription standards for a complete 13-exercise full-body workout using home gym cable stations, resistance bands, and chest fly machines.

2

General Safety Guidelines

  • Mandatory Warm-Up: Complete 5–10 minutes of light dynamic movement before initiating loaded exercises.
  • Equipment Inspection: Verify carabiner locks, ankle strap buckles, cable integrity, and weight stack pin insertion.
  • Controlled Tempo: Perform reps at a controlled 2-second concentric (pushing/pulling) and 2-second eccentric (lowering) cadence.
  • Respiratory Control: Exhale during exertion and inhale during the controlled return motion.
  • Joint Safety: Immediately terminate any exercise if acute joint or lumbar spinal sharp pain is experienced.

Purpose

This SOP details every static cool-down stretch referenced in the Combined Warm-Up + Workout + Stretch Schedule (SOP-GYM-COMBO-01). Static stretches are held (not bounced or moving) and are performed AFTER training, when muscles are already warm — they improve flexibility, help reduce post-workout stiffness, and gradually return the heart rate to normal.

2

General Guidelines

  • Perform static stretches only after your workout, once muscles are warm.
  • Ease into each stretch slowly until you feel gentle tension — never pain.
  • Do not bounce — hold each stretch still for the full duration.
  • Breathe normally and try to relax the muscle further with each exhale.
  • Hold each stretch for the time indicated (typically 20–30 seconds), and repeat on both sides where applicable.
  • Stop immediately if you feel sharp, shooting, or joint pain.
